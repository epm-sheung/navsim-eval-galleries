NAVSIM · navtest (v1 PDMS) & navhard (v2 EPDMS)

Scoreboard

Every method this project evaluated, plus every published baseline it is compared against. PUBLISHED is the paper/leaderboard figure; OURS is this pipeline's own run, traced to a per-scene CSV or SLURM log. A method not evaluated under a given metric prints a literal — — nothing here is interpolated, estimated, or left blank to imply zero. Ranked high to low by v1 PDMS (the column nearly every published method reports, and the only one where the whole field is comparable) — rows with no v1 figure sort to the bottom, never as zero. Every method's backbone/config is printed under its name so the ranking is auditable rather than implicitly apples-to-apples: DriveSuprim's 93.5 is ViT-Large, most others are ResNet-34, ours is a frozen DINOv2 ViT-S with a small head — not like-for-like, and a bare rank invites the wrong read.

Swatches match the colour a method uses in the Scenes tab checkboxes/canvas and in the Distributions charts — same hex values throughout. Hover a published figure for its source citation.
v1 PDMS and v2 EPDMS are different metrics on different splits — never compare a number across the two halves of this table. v1 PDMS is 2 multiplicative gates over 5 weighted subscores, scored on navtest (12,146 scenes, one-stage). v2 EPDMS is 4 multiplicative gates over 9 weighted subscores, scored on navhard (5,912 scenes, two-stage: real stage-1 sensors → synthetic stage-2 perturbation). Every published baseline in this table's left half (v1) is a v1 PDMS figure; every one in the right half (v2) is a v2 EPDMS figure — there is no baseline that is directly comparable across the split.
