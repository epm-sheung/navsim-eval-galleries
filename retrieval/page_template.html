Which representation finds scenes that drive the same way?

Each query is a scene’s t0 camera frame — the last frame of the past. Every representation retrieves its ten nearest scenes from other driving logs, and we score them on how closely those scenes’ next 4 seconds match the query’s.

Why our model looked barely better than stock DINOv2

We were measuring the wrong thing. Our model keeps DINOv2 frozen; what it learns lives in the patch-token aggregator on top, whose readout is the scene vector the waypoint head uses. Comparing CLS tokens compared two trunks, and the “fine-tuned” trunk was tuned on a different dataset (PAV, triplet loss, blocks 10–11 only), not by our driving objective. Measured where the learning actually is, the gap opens up:

The full readout is handed past motion and the driving command, so we checked how much of that is bookkeeping: those inputs alone, with no image, reach __CTRL_ADE__ m. The readout beats that, so the image adds real information on top.

DrivoR’s gate removes dictionary entries that never activate on navtest, so its compressed rows equal its uncompressed ones and are merged in the scoreboard. Drive-JEPA’s gate removes live signal and still costs no retrieval quality. Both measured on navtest only.

How it was built

Stock / fine-tuned trunk: DINOv2 ViT-S/14 on the cached 504×280 frame, CLS or mean of patch tokens. The fine-tuned trunk is the one inside waypoint_navsim_ft/epoch013-best.ckpt: 30 of 175 tensors changed, all in blocks 10–11 (21–33% relative change on those weights).

Our model: patch_tokens_film_navsim (navtest v1 PDMS 0.8302), frozen stock trunk + trained aggregator; the descriptor is the aggregator readout token. “Image only” zeroes past motion and fixes the command; the full row feeds the real inputs exactly as the eval agent does.

DrivoR / Drive-JEPA: rebuilt from the shared checkpoints and fed their own input — DrivoR at 1148×672, Drive-JEPA as a two-frame 512×256 clip.

Controls: past motion (4 poses + velocity/acceleration), with and without the command, z-scored, no image.

Chance: ten random scenes from other logs. Generated __GEN__.
