Green tiles: how many of the top 10 drive like the query

For each of the __NQ__ queries, count how many of its ten retrieved scenes have a 4 s future within 2 m ADE of the query’s — the gallery’s green border — then average. At least one is the share of queries where any of the ten qualifies. Neighbours are chosen by scene features alone; the trajectory is only used afterwards to grade them.

__INVENTORY__
How it was built

Our method. Trunk rows: DINOv2 ViT-S/14 on the cached 504×280 t0 frame. Readout rows: the scene aggregator of the trunk 2×2 models (DINOWaypointCLS, navtrain, 4,000 steps, seed 42), cell C with the stock trunk and cell D with the fine-tuned one — cell D’s trunk is bit-identical to the fine-tuned trunk in the rows above. Past motion joins only after the readout, so the readout is image-only.

DrivoR. Rebuilt from the shared checkpoints; the original backbone is the same LoRA model with the LoRA branch set to zero, verified against timm’s release (174/175 tensors identical, the last being timm’s own pos_embed resample, equal bit-for-bit). The +SAE model applies its gate inside the forward pass at blocks[10], as it runs. Readout = mean of the 16 scene tokens the planner reads.

Drive-JEPA. Original = vitl_merge_3dataset_e50.pt from the Drive-JEPA release; released and +SAE models from the shared checkpoints; the +SAE gate is applied on the projector as it runs.

Queries: 2,000 evenly spaced over the corpus; 30 display queries stratified by direction and speed. Neighbours always from other driving logs. Generated __GEN__.
