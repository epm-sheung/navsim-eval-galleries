Where the SAE changes what gets retrieved

For each method, every one of the __NC__ navtest scenes is used as a retrieval query with both its original backbone and its SAE version. score = |greenorig − greensae|, where green is how many of the top __TOPK__ neighbours (cosine similarity, other driving logs only) have a 4 s future within __GREEN_M__ m ADE of the query’s. Queries are ranked by that score, ties broken by |Δmean ADE| then by token; __NSAMPLE__ queries are sampled at evenly spaced ranks (0% = biggest disagreement, 100% = smallest) for inspection below.

Reading the tiles

Under every neighbour, its 4 s future is drawn bold in teal, with the query’s as a faint dashed grey reference, each as 8 waypoints at 0.5 s spacing, with the ADE between them. A green border marks a neighbour whose future is within __GREEN_M__ m ADE of the query’s. The lateral axis is stretched about 3× so turns stay visible; ADE is unaffected.
