Where the SAE changes what gets retrieved

For each method, every one of the __NC__ navtest scenes is used as a retrieval query with both its original backbone and its SAE version. Δgreen = greensae − greenorig (green = how many of the top __TOPK__ neighbours — cosine similarity, other driving logs only — have a 4 s future within __GREEN_M__ m ADE of the query’s), signed from −__TOPK__ to +__TOPK__: positive means the SAE retrieves more green neighbours (SAE better), negative means worse. Queries are ranked by Δgreen descending — rank 1 is where the SAE helps most, the last rank is where it hurts most — ties broken by the signed (mean ADEorig − mean ADEsae) descending (positive = SAE better there too), then by token. __NSAMPLE__ queries are sampled at evenly spaced ranks (0% = SAE helps most, 100% = SAE hurts most) for inspection below.

Method:
Δgreen distribution over all __NC__ queries (−__TOPK__..+__TOPK__, 0 = tie), current sample marked
