Score distributions

Every method that has been scored, one card each, one chart at a time (switch below). Each chart is that method's OWN distribution only — no other method's score is drawn on it, just this method's own mean (dashed line). Click a bar to reveal a linked example scene from this package's 210 rendered clips: its clip, its score, and a link across to the Scenes tab. Many bins will have no rendered example — the charts cover the full population (12,146 navtest scenes / 225 navhard groups) while clips exist for only 210 selected scenes — and the panel says so plainly rather than substituting a neighbouring scene. See distributions/scripts/export_chart_data.py's module docstring for the exact linkage rules (v1 can link; v2 structurally cannot — see that docstring).

v1 and v2 are not comparable — two different metrics on two different splits, confused before in this project. v1 PDMS: 2 multiplicative gates over 5 subscores, one-stage, scored on navtest (12,146 scenes). v2 EPDMS: 4 multiplicative gates over 9 subscores, two-stage (real stage-1 sensors → synthetic stage-2 perturbation), scored on navhard (5,912 scenes → 225 scenario groups, see docs/NAVSIM_EVAL.md Trap 8). Switching below only changes which chart set is shown; it never reconciles the two numbers.
